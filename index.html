The Short Version

It started with a horse barn and a stubborn love of animals.

Paradigm Boxers began the way most good breeding programs do — not as a business plan, but as a life spent around animals. Years working in the medical field taught a certain discipline about health tracking and record-keeping; years around horses and, eventually, boxers, taught the rest.

Today the dogs share the house, the yard, and the school run with our kids. Puppies are whelped in the home, weighed and logged daily, and never see a kennel run until they're grown enough to enjoy one.

How we do things

01

Health before anything

OFA hips and elbows, DM (degenerative myelopathy), echocardiogram, and thyroid testing on every parent before a single pairing is even considered.

02

Raised in the house

Whelping happens in our home. Puppies hear dishwashers, doorbells, and kids before they hear anything else.

03

One litter per dog, per year

Never more. Across the whole program that's roughly 6–8 litters a year — enough that every puppy still gets tracked, weighed, and handled daily.

04

Retirement, not burnout

Each girl has 3–4 litters in her lifetime, then retires to her own forever family. We don't breed a dog to exhaustion.
