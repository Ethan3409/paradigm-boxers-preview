How the waitlist works

01

Reach out

Fill out the contact form and tell us about your household, experience with dogs, and what you're hoping for.

02

A conversation, not a form letter

We follow up personally — usually a call or a visit — before anyone is added to a litter list.

03

Deposit secures your spot

A refundable-until-birth deposit (placeholder policy) holds your place in pick order once a litter is confirmed.

04

Pick order & go-home day

We temperament test every puppy at 49 days old — modeled on the Volhard Puppy Aptitude Test. That's the age a puppy is neurologically complete but hasn't yet had time to learn behaviours that mask their natural temperament; testing earlier (or later) gives a less reliable read. Picks are made by deposit order once results are in, and puppies go home at 8 weeks.
