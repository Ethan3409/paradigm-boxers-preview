Why It Stayed Small

A day job in medicine changed how we track a litter.

What we stand behind

The non-negotiables, whether it's a quiet year or a busy one.

01

Full health testing

OFA hips and elbows, a DM (degenerative myelopathy) clearance, echocardiogram, annual heart Holter monitoring, and thyroid panels on every dog we breed from — results shared on request.

02

Temperament first

Structure and bloodlines matter, but a dog with a poor temperament never enters our breeding program, full stop.

03

Home-raised, always

Whelping and every week after happen inside our home, underfoot. Puppies don't move out to the dog run until they're about six months old and big enough to enjoy it safely.

04

Early socialization

Kids, noise, car rides, and handling exercises start well before puppies go to their new families.

05

Lifetime support

Every family who takes home a Paradigm puppy can call us — for advice, for training questions, for anything — for the dog's whole life.

06

Honest limits

Each girl has one litter a year, at most, and retires to her forever family after 3–4 litters in her lifetime. If we don't have a puppy for you right now, we'll say so.
